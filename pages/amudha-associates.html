02 / THE AMUDHA STORY

BUILT ON
EXPERIENCE.
MADE FOR
TOMORROW.

Amudha Associates is a Coimbatore-based manufacturer and supplier of industrial and outdoor shading solutions, serving residential, commercial and industrial requirements since 1997.

From custom-sized PVC strip curtains and polycarbonate canopies to foldable tents, retractable awnings and tensile structures, our approach is rooted in quality materials, practical performance and solutions built around the customer.
