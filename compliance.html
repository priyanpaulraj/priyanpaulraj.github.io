Tax Compliance Logic

Key GST Principles

01

Product GST: Always charged by Merchant & filed via their return. Applies to both COD & PG.

02

Delivery GST: Liability follows the invoice owner. If Merchant bills, Merchant collects. If 3rd-party bills, they collect.

03

Payment Gateway: PG charges MDR + GST to the Merchant (B2B), never directly to the customer.

04

Handling Charges: COD fees are taxable as service income by the party collecting them (usually the courier).
