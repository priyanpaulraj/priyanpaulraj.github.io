Simple Tax Flow Guide

How it Works

01

The Product: The Shop Owner always collects the tax for the item and pays it to the government.

02

Delivery Fees: Whoever asks the customer for the delivery money is the one responsible for the tax on that fee.

03

Card/Online Payments: The Payment App (like Razorpay) charges the Shop Owner a small fee. The customer doesn't pay this extra tax.

04

Cash on Delivery: If a courier charges a "handling fee" for collecting cash, that fee has its own tax.
